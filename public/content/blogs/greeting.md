# Why I built this Digital Garden?

Welp everything come to me in a weird way. Even this digital garden idea.

You guys know that every professional OG internet user or developer back in when .COM is still a big bubble? Most of them hanging around blogs and forums website together.

I'm a bit of a **later generation**, I didn't experiences the full of that internet culture. But the concept idea of having a personal digital garden, where can I write blogs, developer document, my information,... in one central places in a very **<u>me</u>** way instead of doing it in multiple social media platform is a very cool and professional way.

And combining the admiration to the OG internet at those Y2K, and admiration to the personal "me" style, also adding the love of a professional touch. Those all combining to this website, the digital garden of myself.
